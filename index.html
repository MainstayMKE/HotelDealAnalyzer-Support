Frequently asked questions

Where are my hotel projects saved?
Projects are stored locally on your device. Use My Projects → Backup & Restore → Export Projects to save a separate copy.

How do I move my projects?
Export a project backup (.json) to Files and import it on the destination device. For an individual editable project, use Share and the .hdeal file format.

How do I restore lifetime access?
Open My Hotel Projects → Access & Purchases → Restore Purchases. Use the Apple Account associated with your purchase. Internet connectivity may be needed for verification.

Can I view all pages in a PDF?
Use the report's Open / Share PDF control, or save the report to Files and open it there to browse its pages.

Is this financial advice?
No. The app provides preliminary financial estimates based on inputs and assumptions you provide. It does not constitute an appraisal, investment advice, or loan approval.
