Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026

Hotel Deal Analyzer is designed to help users evaluate hotel acquisitions and financing scenarios. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information you enter

Hotel names, acquisition assumptions, financing inputs, saved projects, and optional report preparer names are stored locally on your device. The app performs financial calculations and creates PDF reports on your device. We do not operate a server that receives these hotel project details during normal app use.

Sharing and backups

You control whether to export a backup, share an editable project, or send a PDF report. When you choose to share or save a file, the destination app or service you select (such as Files, Mail, Messages, or a cloud-storage provider) handles the copy according to its own privacy practices.

Purchases

The 7-day trial and lifetime unlock are handled using Apple's In-App Purchase system. Apple processes purchase authentication and transactions. Hotel Deal Analyzer checks purchase entitlements to determine feature access. We do not request or store your payment-card details. Apple's handling of transaction data is governed by Apple's own privacy policies.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

The current app does not include developer-operated advertising or analytics services, and we do not use hotel project information for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Retention and deletion

Saved projects and preferences remain on your device until you delete them or remove the app's data. Projects moved to Recently Deleted may be restored for up to 30 days while their local data remains available. Removing app data can permanently erase local records; export a backup first if you want to retain them. Files you have shared or exported must be managed separately in the destination where you saved them.

Children

Hotel Deal Analyzer is a general-purpose financial calculation tool and is not specifically designed for children.

Changes to this policy

If our information-handling practices change, we will update this policy and its effective date as appropriate.
