Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026

Mental Math Pal is an arithmetic practice application for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains the data handled by the app and how you can contact the developer.

Practice information and local storage

The app saves practice sessions, question results, performance statistics, unfinished practice checkpoints, selected options, sound and appearance preferences, and the local trial start time on your device. These records are used to provide the learning experience, support session recovery, show progress, and determine trial access.

The current app does not require a Mental Math Pal account. We do not operate a separate developer server to receive your arithmetic answers or practice history during normal use. Information stored on a device may be included in device-level backups depending on your operating-system and backup settings.

Optional microphone and speech recognition

Some multiplication practice may offer an optional voice-answer feature. If you choose to use it, the app requests microphone and speech-recognition permission and uses Apple's speech-recognition capabilities to interpret your spoken answer. Speech processing may be performed by Apple according to the service and device configuration. You can decline or change permission in device Settings and use the number pad instead.

We do not run a separate speech-recording server, and the app is not designed to save or publish recordings of spoken answers. Apple's handling of any speech data is subject to its applicable privacy information.

Purchases and trial

Mental Math Pal offers a seven-day trial and an optional one-time lifetime unlock through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Apple processes payment authentication and transactions. The app checks verified StoreKit purchase entitlements to determine access and does not ask for or store your payment-card information.

Advertising, analytics, and tracking

The current app does not include third-party advertising or developer-operated analytics or tracking SDKs. We do not use students' arithmetic answers for targeted advertisements or cross-app tracking.

Contacting support

If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address and the contents of your message. We use it to respond to your request. Please do not include sensitive personal details about children, account passwords, or payment-card data.

Children and parents

Mental Math Pal is intended to support arithmetic practice by learners, including children. No student account, name, or school information is required to practice. Parents or guardians should assist children with purchases and any support correspondence.

Retention and deletion

Locally stored results and settings generally remain until removed through supported in-app controls, by clearing the app's device data, or by uninstalling the app. Uninstalling or clearing device data may permanently erase local practice history. Any separate system backups or support emails must be managed through their respective services.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its information-handling practices change. The effective date above will be revised when appropriate.
