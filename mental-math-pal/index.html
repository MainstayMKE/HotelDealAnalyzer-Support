Frequently asked questions

How do I begin practicing?
Choose Addition, Subtraction, Multiplication, or Division on the Home screen. Select the available practice options and follow the prompts.

How does Written Multiplication work?
Enter each partial product using the number pad, then calculate and enter the final total yourself. The worksheet accepts supported partial-product sequences and provides feedback after submission.

How do the Addition and Subtraction arrows work?
Directional arrows help indicate the next column to solve. Use the on-screen Arrow Help when available for an explanation.

What do I enter when rounding a decimal division answer?
Enter the two digits that should appear after the decimal point in the final answer. Tap the information/help control beside the rounding instruction for an example.

Where is my practice history?
Open Results & Progress. Practice results are saved locally on your device. They are not automatically synchronized between devices by a Mental Math Pal account.

How long is the trial?
New installations include a seven-day trial as displayed in the app. Continued access is offered through a one-time lifetime in-app purchase; there is no recurring subscription.

How do I restore a purchase?
Open Settings → Access & Purchases → Restore Purchases and use the Apple Account associated with the purchase. An internet connection may be needed for Apple to verify the transaction.

What happens after a verified lifetime purchase?
The app recognizes lifetime ownership and removes trial/purchase reminders from Home. Your access status and Restore Purchases remain in Settings.

Is speech input required?
No. Where a voice-answer option is available, microphone and speech-recognition access are optional. You can continue practicing with the number pad instead.
